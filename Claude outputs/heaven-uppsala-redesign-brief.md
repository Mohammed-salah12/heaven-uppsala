# Heaven Uppsala — redesign brief (translated from Swedish)

Source: shared ChatGPT conversation, "Skapa hemsidemockup" ("Create homepage mockup"). Translated in full below; nothing summarized or cut.

## The core idea

I've gone through the current site and see one important change we should make: the new homepage shouldn't present Heaven as a restaurant with a few side services. It should present Heaven as a destination with several experiences under one roof.

The current site is very churrasco-focused, while conference/party sits separately, and the café/wine bar, club, and Måla & Skåla ("Paint & Toast") don't get the central role they need. At the same time there's good material to reuse — the churrasco description, the Brazilian drinks, the conference/event capacity.

**My proposal: THIS IS HEAVEN**

I'd build the homepage around the idea we worked on earlier:

> **THIS IS HEAVEN**
> Eat. Drink. Meet. Celebrate. Stay.

That makes "Heaven" the master brand, and the different concepts become experiences within Heaven, not separate brands.

## First screen — Hero

Large, cinematic image/video of the venue — ideally a sequence that cuts between fire/churrasco → wine/coffee → people → DJ → event.

> HEAVEN
>
> **THIS IS HEAVEN**
>
> More than a restaurant.
> A place to eat, drink, meet & celebrate.
>
> In the heart of Uppsala, Heaven brings together food, wine, café, nightlife, art and events under one roof.
>
> [DISCOVER HEAVEN]   [BOOK]

I wouldn't open the homepage with prices, menus or opening hours. First, we sell Heaven.

## ONE PLACE. MANY EXPERIENCES.

This is the actual core of the new site.

On desktop: five large visual cards. Hovering a card brings it to life with video. On mobile: scroll vertically.

**🔥 HEAVEN CHURRASCO**
Brazilian Churrasco Rodizio
*Fire. Meat. Community.*

Experience Brazilian churrasco in the heart of Uppsala. Our *passadores* come to the table with skewers and carve the meat straight onto your plate — just like a traditional Brazilian churrascaria.

Rodizio Thursday–Saturday. For groups, we offer churrasco on other days too, by booking.

`[EXPERIENCE CHURRASCO]` `[BOOK A TABLE]`

*(Builds on one of the strongest parts of the current site: meat served from the skewer straight at the table.)*

**☕🍷 NEXT TO HEAVEN**
Coffee • Matcha • Wine • Social
*From the first coffee to the last glass of wine.*

Next to Heaven is our café and wine bar — a relaxed meeting place in central Uppsala.

Specialty coffee, matcha, iced tea, milkshakes and drinks inspired by what's trending right now. Later in the day the focus shifts to wine, with a large selection by the glass.

We serve smørrebrød and other small plates alongside the drinks.

`[DISCOVER NEXT TO HEAVEN]` `[SEE MENU]`

*(The nice thing here is we can communicate that the same space changes through the day, rather than trying to define it as just a café or just a wine bar.)*

**🎧 CLUB HEAVEN**
When dinner ends, Heaven continues.
*Music. Cocktails. People. Late nights.*

Club Heaven is our world of nightlife, DJs, club nights and happenings.

An evening at Heaven doesn't have to end after dinner.

Stay for the night.

`[CLUB HEAVEN]` `[UPCOMING EVENTS]`

*(I'd integrate real Instagram/TikTok content and a proper What's On calendar here later — important since the club program changes week to week.)*

**🥂 PARTY • EVENT • CONFERENCE**
Your event. Our House.

From the morning meeting to the party late into the evening.

Heaven offers flexible spaces for conferences, corporate events, birthdays, weddings, dinners and private parties — in central Uppsala.

The existing site already has a lot of useful material here: conference setup, stage, sound system, microphones, Wi-Fi, and flexible seating. It also states conference capacity up to 300 people and describes options for larger events.

`[PARTY & EVENT]` `[CONFERENCE]` `[SEND REQUEST]`

Here I'd mainly want one button:

> **PLAN MY EVENT →**

Leading to a very simple form: date, number of people, event type, food/drink, contact details.

**🎨 MÅLA & SKÅLA** ("Paint & Toast")
Paint. Toast. Socialize.

An evening doesn't have to be just about dinner.

Book a Måla & Skåla evening at Heaven and combine creativity, wine, food and company.

Come alone, with friends, or book a private event for your company or group.

No experience needed — just the desire to create.

`[SEE UPCOMING DATES]` `[BOOK A PRIVATE EVENT]`

## WHAT'S HAPPENING IN HEAVEN?

I think this part is very important.

Instead of asking the guest to understand every concept, we show what's actually happening:

**THURSDAY** — 🔥 Churrasco Rodizio

**FRIDAY** — 🔥 Churrasco Rodizio · 🍷 Wine & After Work · 🎧 Club Heaven

**SATURDAY** — ☕ Next to Heaven · 🔥 Churrasco Rodizio · 🎨 Måla & Skåla · 🎧 Club Heaven

In the finished build this should be dynamic, so you can post things like:

- DJ Devine – Saturday 22:00
- Måla & Skåla – 17 September
- Wine Wednesday
- Brazilian Night
- Champagne Tasting

without anyone needing to rebuild the website.

## FROM DAY TO NIGHT

A wide video here.

16:00 — Wine
18:00 — Dinner
21:00 — Cocktails
23:00 — Club

Then:

> **ONE PLACE. ALL DAY. THIS IS HEAVEN.**

This is really the positioning I think sets you apart the most.

## Then the booking section

Instead of a single "Book a table" button, which the current homepage has, the new Heaven would ask:

> **WHAT DO YOU WANT TO EXPERIENCE?**
>
> 🔥 Book Churrasco
> ☕ Book a table / Next to Heaven
> 🎧 Club / Guest list
> 🎨 Måla & Skåla
> 🥂 Party & private event
> 💼 Conference

That's a much better information architecture for the business you actually run.

## Navigation

I'd significantly simplify the current, fairly traditional Wix-style navigation. Right now Hem, Bakfickan, Konferens, Festvåning, Matmeny and Drinkmeny sit as separate items.

New:

> HEAVEN | CHURRASCO | NEXT TO HEAVEN | CLUB | EVENT & CONFERENCE | MÅLA & SKÅLA | WHAT'S ON | BOOK

With BOOK as a clear button, far right.

## One more thing I'd change

I'd avoid the name "Restaurang Heaven" in overall communication.

Use:

> **HEAVEN UPPSALA**

and let the restaurant be one part of Heaven.

That makes the brand logical even when someone comes at 11 for matcha, 14 for lunch, 18 for wine, 20 for churrasco, 23 for a DJ — or rents the whole party floor.

It's significantly more scalable than the current restaurant-first positioning.

## Proposed next step (from the original doc)

The next step should be turning this into an actual visual desktop mockup of the homepage — with the Heaven feel, typography, hero, navigation and the five concept sections — so we can see what the new site could actually look like before discussing tech/platform. A mobile view with the same content was also requested as a follow-up.

## What this means for the current build

This is a full brand repositioning, not a copy tweak — it touches the homepage structure, navigation, the booking flow, and introduces three concepts that don't exist on the site yet (Next to Heaven, Club Heaven, Måla & Skåla), plus a dynamic weekly schedule and a rename from "Restaurang Heaven" to "Heaven Uppsala". Worth deciding together how much of this to build now versus treat as a longer-term plan — happy to scope it either way once you say which pieces to start with.
